Privacy & playback

A simple site.
A few things to know.

On this website

This static website has no account system, contact form, newsletter, shop, custom analytics or advertising scripts. Its own code does not set cookies or store browsing preferences. The hosting provider may process connection data such as IP addresses and server logs under its own privacy policy.

YouTube players

Listening pages embed YouTube using its privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player. Loading a listening page makes requests to YouTube; playing the video can involve further data processing and storage by Google. Privacy-enhanced does not mean no data is exchanged. YouTube’s own service terms and privacy controls apply.

If you prefer not to load an embedded player, stay on the library page and choose the channel link when you are ready to visit YouTube. Videos never autoplay on this site.

External links

Links to YouTube and Pinterest take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. No view counts or outbound clicks are measured by custom tracking code on this website.

Images and sound

The displayed artwork and the linked ambiences are AI-created works assembled for MindVerseChronicles. They do not represent recordings of identifiable real places. This website does not collect personal testimonials or promise health benefits.
